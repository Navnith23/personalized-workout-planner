Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

To generate your personalized fitness plan we collect:

  • Basic personal information (age, sex, height, weight) — used only to compute BMI and contextualize your plan.
  • Health screening answers (yes/no questions about symptoms, injuries, medical conditions).
  • Fitness test results and training history.
  • Weekly check-in data (sessions completed, perceived difficulty, recovery).

How we use it

  • All data is used exclusively to generate and adapt your fitness plan.
  • We do not sell, share, or disclose your health data to third parties.
  • Data is stored on PythonAnywhere servers. Database files are not publicly accessible.

Health data disclaimer

This planner provides general fitness guidance and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you have a health condition, consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any exercise programme.

Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting the site administrator.

Contact

For data requests or privacy concerns, contact the site administrator via PythonAnywhere.
