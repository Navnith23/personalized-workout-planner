SECURITY & TELEMETRY PROTOCOL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 // Kinetic Sync v3.4

1. Data Collection Scope

To calibrate personalized athletic programs and autoregulated microcycles, we collect:

  • Biometric Markers: Age, biological sex, height, weight — utilized exclusively to compute baseline energy expenditure and contextualize volume recommendations.
  • Safety & Health Screening: PAR-Q screening inputs (joint symptoms, cardiovascular history, previous musculoskeletal injuries).
  • Athletic Capacity & Training History: Fitness test benchmarks, training background, and equipment availability.
  • Weekly Biofeedback & Telemetry: Perceived session difficulty (RPE), energy levels, systemic recovery scores, and optional heart rate metrics (resting and workout bpm).

2. Use of Telemetry Data

  • All personal telemetry is utilized solely to calibrate and adjust your training volume, exercise selection, and microcycle progression.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal or health metrics with third-party advertising networks.
  • All data is securely encrypted in transit and stored in protected databases.

3. Medical Advisory Disclaimer

KINETIC//SYNC provides structured athletic conditioning and strength guidance. It is not a medical device, diagnosis tool, or substitute for individualized clinical consultation. Athletes with pre-existing conditions must consult a physician prior to starting or modifying physical exertion protocols.

4. Data Retention & Athlete Rights

Your account and telemetry records are maintained for the duration of your athletic membership. You may export or permanently delete your profile and all historical logs at any time via your account settings.
